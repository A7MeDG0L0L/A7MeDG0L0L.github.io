XO Clash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

XO Clash is published by A.GDev ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal data ourselves. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your scores and settings stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the advertising SDK collects

The app shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google collects and processes this data on its own servers:
Google uses this data to serve, measure and personalize ads, for analytics, and for fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS).

  • Device identifiers: advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data about the ads SDK

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
